### 🎲 Design Outcome: Scratch Company prints Deploys Exhausted (2026-10-23)

- **Question:** The user proposed replacing the game rule "a Scratch Company always enters Exhausted" (`FR-ZON-005`, ruled as `KIM-S01` in FRD 01 v3.3) with the keyword **Deploys Exhausted** printed on the face-down Scratch Company face. Knock-on questions checked: whether `FR-KWD-002l` applies to a scratch (Scratch N is not the Deploy action), *Forced Mutation* (`FR-ENT-103`), effects that remove abilities, and the `FR-KWD-002m` and Immediate Readiness (`FR-SET-004`) wording.
- **Rulings:**

  | Ruling | Settles | Cards |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | `KIM-S01` (revised) | A new Scratch Company enters Exhausted because its face prints **Deploys Exhausted**, not because of a separate game rule. | Scratch Company face; every Scratch source |
  | `KIM-S05` | A scratch is a unit deploying (the top card of the deck, face-down, as a 1/1 Scratch Company). Abilities that watch for a unit deploying see it. It is not the Deploy action: no cost, no deploy item on the stack. | Every Scratch source; future "whenever a unit deploys" cards |
  | `KIM-S06` | Whether an asset has Deploys Exhausted is decided as it enters, counting effects already in force that would grant or remove it once it is on the battlefield. An effect that removes it from Scratch Companies, or that makes them deploy Readied on top of it, lets them enter Readied. | None in M2; future Animus or Soulless payoffs |

- **Decisions (user, 2026-10-23):**
  - **`KIM-S01`:** "Scratch Company should have 'Deploys Exhausted' on it instead of a game rule." At the table it is "'scratch companies deploy exhausted' as a rule to remember. It's not facedown cards as a whole."
  - **`KIM-S05`:** "scratching is essentially 'deploy the top card of your deck facedown as a 1/1 scratch company unit', so yes it counts as a unit deploying."
  - **`KIM-S06`:** "a card that wipes the scratch company 'deploy exhausted' keyword would be costed so that it doesn't have that same 'run away with the game on earlier turns' effect, it would be a finisher / tempo swinging card mid to late game the way Rapturous Sermon is meant to be." On its wording: "The hypothetical finisher would be worded as 'Scratch Company units deploy readied' or something which applies on top of the 'Deploys Exhausted'." Context: "entering exhausted is a balancing mechanic. Scratch companies entering readied lead to turns where someone could empty their entire hand out and be super far ahead. Things keying on Scratch Company is an open space that Animus currently dip into and probably Soulless will dip into eventually."
- **Cards affected:**
  - *Scratch Company* (`data/cards/units-and-structures-common.csv`): its card text changes. It is a match-generated face, banned from decks.
  - Sources of Scratch Companies, with the same outcome in every M2 game: the Headquarters' Deploy Scratch Company action, *Animoceps Rager* (HQ), *Spoil Forest Creeper*, *Fungal Evangelist*, *Despoiler Larva*, *Titanic Spore Carrier*, *Animoceps Spores* (Animus), and *Devilhorn Scouting Party* (Path).
  - *Forced Mutation* (Animus, M2): the outcome is unchanged. A mutated unit keeps its readiness, because Deploys Exhausted applies only on entering.
  - No M2 card removes keywords or watches another unit deploying, so `KIM-S05` and `KIM-S06` change no M2 game.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - *Keep it a game rule, worded as the table says it* (game-designer's recommendation, so that no ability-removal effect can switch it off): the user wants the printed keyword, and a card that removes it is a deliberate, costed late-game finisher.
  - *All face-down entries enter Exhausted:* the table rule is about Scratch Companies, not face-down cards.
  - *Make the Scratch Company's Deploys Exhausted impossible to remove:* that is a special case, and the user wants cards that wipe it to be possible.
