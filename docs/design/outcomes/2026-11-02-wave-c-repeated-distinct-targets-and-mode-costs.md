### ❓ Open Ruling Question: repeated targets on a timeout, two "chosen" slots in one effect, and mode costs (2026-11-02)

- **Question:**
  1. **Repeated targets on a timeout (*Steel Rain*; board directive #59 (2)).** `SVY-R4` makes each discarded card its own target choice, and the same asset may be chosen more than once. When the player does not answer those choices in time (`FR-MOD-001` §6), which assets take the damage?
     - (a) Every repetition picks the first legal asset in §6's fixed order, so all the damage goes to one asset (3 discards → 6 damage to it).
     - (b) The repetitions walk down the fixed order: the first legal asset, then the second, and so on, starting again at the top when the list runs out. This is what the kernel does today for any decision that allows repeats.
  2. **Mode costs (*Ambush Tactics*; board directive #61, fifth question).** Is a mode legal only if its costs can also be paid? Are a mode, its targets and its costs all locked on placement as one step, rather than one after another?
  3. **"Another chosen unit" (*Ambush Tactics* mode 3; design note Open Question 16).** In *"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to another chosen unit"*, the two picks must differ.
     - (a) Is the mode legal only if two different units are legal at the same time (one for each slot), so a battlefield with a single unit makes mode 3 illegal?
     - (b) Is the second slot offered every legal unit except the first slot's pick?
     - (c) On a timeout, is the dealer the first unit in fixed order and the receiver the next different one?
  4. **Two "chosen" clauses without "another" (*Ensnaring Mist*, *Pincer Tactics*; design note Open Question 16).** May the targets of two separate clauses in one effect be the same entity?
     - *Ensnaring Mist*: may the unit that is moved to Reserves also be the unit that gets -1 power? Or is the second "chosen unit" the same target as the first, so there is only one choice?
     - *Pincer Tactics*: may the "chosen non-Scratch asset" be one of the "up to 2 chosen allied units", so that it takes 1 + 2 damage?

- **Current FRD 01 & engine state:**
  - Question 1: FRD 01 v3.19 `SVY-R4` (line 86) makes the per-card choices independent and allows the same entity. `FR-MOD-001` §6 (line 388) says a timed-out mandatory target takes "the first ones in fixed order", which, applied to each choice on its own, gives (a). The kernel instead cycles through the fixed order for a decision with `Repeats` (`pkg/engine/choice.go:203-207`, `fixedOrderPicks`), which gives (b). No card uses repeated targets yet, so neither reading is pinned by a test. Note: the fixed order starts with the choosing player's own Headquarters, so under (a) and (b) alike a timed-out *Steel Rain* aims at least its first 2 damage at its own Headquarters. That is today's `SVY-R16` rule for every "chosen asset" slot and isn't new here.
  - Question 2: `SVY-R28` (line 21) makes a mode legal by its targets alone. No in-scope modal card (*Ambush Tactics*, *Rogue Fleshshaper*) has a cost on a mode, and *Ambush Tactics*' Tactics cost belongs to the card. A Maneuver is enactable only if its additional costs are payable, then its modes or targets are legal (`pkg/engine/placement.go:119-132`, `placeable`). `legalModes` (`placement.go:136-144`) checks targets only. The kernel already chooses a mode's targets right after the mode, with nothing in between (`chooseModes`, `placement.go:154`).
  - Question 3: `SVY-R11` (line 65) makes the picks of one *"up to N"* clause distinct, and says nothing about two clauses. `SVY-R28` checks each exactly-one slot on its own, so a single unit would make mode 3 legal. Today every slot is chosen independently, and a later slot can name an earlier slot's pick (`pkg/engine/targeting.go:20-36`, `chooseTargets`). Nothing in `ability.Target` marks a slot as "another".
  - Question 4: the same code lets the two slots name the same entity today. Different modes of one Maneuver may already share a unit (`SVY-R25`; board directive #88 test (c), where unit X is both returned and the dealer).
- **Lead's recommendation:**
  1. (a). Each repetition is its own choice (`SVY-R4`), so §6 applies to each one as written, and the rule stays one sentence a player can predict. The kernel's cycling changes in the *Steel Rain* targeting task.
  2. Yes to both. A mode whose cost can't be paid is illegal, just as one whose exactly-one target is missing is (extending `SVY-R28`). The mode, its targets and its costs are committed in one step on placement, as `SVY-R3` already does for targets and `FR-TRN-003a` for a Maneuver's additional cost. No M2 card has a mode cost, so this only fixes the rule for later cards.
  3. Yes to (a), (b) and (c). "Another" names a second, different unit. Checking legality slot by slot would offer a mode that can't be completed, the case `SVY-R29` exists to rule out.
  4. Yes, they may be the same entity, and on *Ensnaring Mist* they are two choices. Only "another" forces a different pick, and a card meaning one target would say "that unit". This matches what different modes may already do (`SVY-R25`) and needs no kernel change.
