### ❓ Open Ruling Question: how "Kill 2 or 4" picks its count (2026-11-16)

- **Question:**
  1. **When is the count chosen, and by what?** "Kill 2 or 4 allied Scratch Company units" has no "chosen", so it is an untargeted selection picked as it resolves (`SVY-R3`). Is the choice between 2 and 4 part of that pick, made as the Maneuver resolves, or is it made when the Maneuver is played (like a mode, `FR-MOD-001` §5), with only the units picked on resolution?
  2. **Fewer matches than the count.** When the Maneuver resolves with 1 or 3 allied Scratch Company units on the battlefield (or fewer than 4 after choosing 4 on play), does the player kill as many as there are (1 kills 1 and deploys nothing; 3 kills 3 and deploys one), or may they only pick a count that can be met exactly (with 3, only 2; with 1, nothing is killed)? `FR-TGT-002` says only that a clause with no match does nothing.
  3. **Timeout.** `FR-MOD-001` §6 gives an untargeted selection "the required number of legal options, the first ones in fixed order". With 2 and 4 both possible, which count does a timed-out player take?
- **Source:** wave H run (`autoloop/m2-wave-h`, session 25), before writing *Mutation Swell*'s tasks. Design note Open Question 25.
- **Cards affected:**
  - *Mutation Swell* (Animus maneuver, `data/cards/maneuvers-animus.csv:12`, 0 Requisition, 3 Tactics): "Kill 2 or 4 allied Scratch Company units. For every 2 units killed this way, deploy a unit card from your discard pile for free." In M2 scope (wave H, on `cards.NotYetExecutable`), in neither starter deck. No other Animus or Path card offers a choice of counts.
- **Current FRD 01 & engine state:**
  - Settled already, so not asked: the kill is untargeted and picked as it resolves, so Ward doesn't apply (`SVY-R3`, `docs/frd/01_Game_Rules_FRD.md:397`, `:404`). A kill that *Shifting Impossibility* replaces still counts toward the deploys (`SVY-R15`, `SVY-R21`, line 528). The deploys pick, as they resolve, among the unit cards in the discard pile after the kills, so a killed Scratch Company's own unit card (face-up there, `FR-ZON-005`) can be deployed. A deploy pick times out to the top of the pile (`SVY-R16`, line 436), and the free deploy enters a printed zone with no costs (`FR-TRN-003a` 1, lines 376–377; no Animus unit prints both zones, so `SVY-R43`'s Reserves timeout doesn't arise). With fewer unit cards in the pile than deploys, the rest do nothing (`FR-TGT-002`'s no-match rule, applied to each deploy).
  - Not settled: line 397 and `FR-MOD-001` §5–§6 (lines 429, 436) describe a selection of one count, an "up to N", or a choice of modes, not a choice between two exact counts. Nothing says when that choice is made, what a shortfall does, or which count a timeout takes. FRD 02's benchmark #14 (`docs/frd/reference/02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md:577`) gives `allowedCounts: [2, 4]` on a `CHOSEN` target, which `SVY-R3` superseded on 2026-10-20.
  - `pkg/engine`: `SubjectPick` (`pkg/engine/targeting.go:166`) picks exactly one, or up to N possibly none (`Step.UpTo`), never an exact count above one or a choice of counts. `stepKill` (`pkg/engine/effects.go:308`) returns how many it killed, and `OpDeployFree` (`pkg/engine/freedeploy.go:21`) deploys picked discard cards for free, but no `ability.Value` reads an earlier step's count ("for every 2 units killed this way").
- **Lead's recommendation:** (1) On resolution, as part of the pick: the card has no "chosen" and no "choose", and the player sees the battlefield when deciding. (2) Kill as many as there are, up to the chosen count, so with 3 units the player may pick 2 or kill all 3 for one deploy, and with 1 the lone unit dies for nothing. That reads the count as a cap, needs no check for whether a count can be met exactly, and the deploys already count the kills actually made (`SVY-R15`). (3) The smaller count, 2, the first ones in fixed order: a timeout should cost the player as little as possible, as an "up to N" timeout selects none.
