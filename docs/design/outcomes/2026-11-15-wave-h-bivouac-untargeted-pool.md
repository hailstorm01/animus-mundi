### ❓ Open Ruling Question: when and how an untargeted pool is distributed (2026-11-15)

- **Question:**
  1. *Hammerhand Bivouac*'s "distribute 2 armor among allied units in the same zone" has no "chosen". Is the split decided **when the trigger is placed** (as targets: Ward and `FR-TGT-003` apply, and an amount on a unit that has become illegal evaporates), or **as it resolves**, among the allied units in the locked zone at that moment (an untargeted selection: no Ward, nothing to evaporate)? `FR-TGT-002`'s Distributed Pool Allocations names *Bivouac* as an example of amounts assigned "to each chosen target upon declaration". `FR-TGT-001`'s `SVY-R44` names *Bivouac* as an untargeted selection that "picks, as it resolves, among the entities in that zone", and `FR-TGT-002`'s Quantifiers say a count without "chosen" doesn't target (`SVY-R3`).
  2. What does a timeout do with a distribution (`FR-MOD-001` §6)? §6's rule for untargeted selections, "the required number of legal options, the first ones in fixed order", gives a count of options, not a split of a pool. With two or more allied units in the zone, does the whole pool go on the first one in fixed order, or one point each to the first ones in turn?
- **Source:** wave H run (`autoloop/m2-wave-h`, session 6), before writing *Hammerhand Bivouac*'s pool and card tasks. Design note Open Question 24.
- **Cards affected:**
  - *Hammerhand Bivouac* (Path structure, `data/cards/units-and-structures-path.csv:17`, deploys to the Main Theater): "At the start of your turn, distribute 2 armor among allied units in the same zone." In M2 scope (wave H, on `cards.NotYetExecutable`), in neither starter deck.
  - *Macrocannon Barrage* (Soulless maneuver, `data/cards/maneuvers-soulless.csv:15`): "Distribute 5 damage among up to 5 chosen units." Outside M2. It is targeted, so its split on declaration isn't in question here. Only its timeout (question 2) would follow the same ruling.
- **Current FRD 01 & engine state:**
  - Settled already, so not asked: start-of-turn triggers resolve completely in the Ready Phase, before the Draw Phase (`FR-TRN-001`, `docs/frd/01_Game_Rules_FRD.md:304`). "The same zone" is the Bivouac's zone when the trigger is placed (`SVY-R44`, line 395). Each unit that receives armor gets a positive whole amount, and the amounts add up to exactly 2 (`FR-TGT-002`, line 406). One allied unit in the zone takes all 2 with nothing asked (`FR-MOD-001` §1), and none takes nothing. Armor gained this way persists (line 506).
  - Not settled: line 406 (on declaration, to chosen targets) and line 395 (on resolution, untargeted) disagree about *Bivouac*, and line 413's evaporation follows from line 406's reading. §6 (line 433) has no rule for splitting a pool.
  - `pkg/engine`: no pool is built yet (design note: "`Pool` for distributed values (`FR-TGT-002`) comes with the task that builds it"). `OpAddArmor` (`pkg/engine/effects.go:214`) adds a fixed `Amount` to each asset of its subject. `SubjectPick` already resolves an untargeted selection on resolution with a `DecidePick` decision and §6's fixed-order fallback (T-0072, T-0076), and `StackItem.Locked` holds a locked zone (T-0205, T-0206).
- **Lead's recommendation:** (1) On resolution. The card has no "chosen", and `SVY-R44` is the newer and more specific ruling. `FR-TGT-002`'s example would then say that a pool among chosen targets is split on declaration, and a pool without "chosen" is split as it resolves among what matches then, so nothing evaporates. (2) The whole pool on the first legal entity in fixed order. That matches `SVY-R32`, where a timed-out repeated target puts every repetition on one entity, and needs no second order.
