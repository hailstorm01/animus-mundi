### 🎲 Design Outcome: Targets removed during resolution, and modal legality (2026-10-30)

- **Question:** What happens when an earlier step or mode of the same effect removes a chosen asset, and what makes a mode legal, when a modal card can be played, and in what order its modes resolve? The user ruled the first part during lead-architect structural review 6, and board directive #82 builds it ([`.agents/tasks/m2/BOARD.md`](../../../.agents/tasks/m2/BOARD.md)). FRD 01 doesn't state it. `FR-TGT-003` checks targets on placement and when resolution starts, and never says what happens when an earlier clause removes a target. The other parts are design note [Open Question 11](../../architecture/m2-ability-model.md#open-questions), which T-0083 left for the user before the *Ambush Tactics* task (wave C). *Ambush Tactics* reaches every part: *"Choose two: Kill chosen unit. / Return chosen unit to its owner's hand. / Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to another chosen unit. / Put chosen unit on top of its owner's deck."*
- **Rulings:**

  | Ruling | Settles | Cards |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | `SVY-R25` | A chosen asset that an earlier step or mode of the same effect removed from the battlefield is no longer that target. The later step does nothing to it, and still acts on the rest of its slot. An asset that leaves and comes back during the same resolution is a new asset and isn't the target either. A step whose chosen assets are all gone did nothing, so an "if you do" step after it doesn't apply (`SVY-R14`). | Ambush Tactics |
  | `SVY-R26` | Between the two verification stages, a step checks only that each chosen asset is still on the battlefield. It doesn't re-check the slot's zone, stat or Ward criteria. | None reaches it in M2. Ensnaring Mist's second slot is any unit. |
  | `SVY-R27` | A chosen asset that deals the damage (*"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to…"*) and is gone, removed by an earlier step or illegal at the second stage, deals nothing. It doesn't use its last-known power: that applies only to the effect's own source (`SVY-R10`). | Ambush Tactics; later Soul Whip (Concordia, M3) |
  | `SVY-R28` | A mode is legal only if each of its exactly-one target slots has a legal entity when the effect is placed. An "up to N" slot never makes it illegal. Only legal modes may be chosen. | Ambush Tactics, Rogue Fleshshaper |
  | `SVY-R29` | A Maneuver can be enacted only if each of its exactly-one target slots has a legal entity on declaration, and a "Choose N" Maneuver only if N of its modes are legal. Otherwise it stays in hand. | Ambush Tactics, and every Maneuver with a "chosen" target |
  | `SVY-R30` | A modal triggered ability is still placed. With fewer than N legal modes it uses every legal one, and with none it is placed with no mode and does nothing. | Rogue Fleshshaper (no M2 case reaches it) |
  | `SVY-R31` | Chosen modes resolve in printed order, not the order they were picked in. | Ambush Tactics, Rogue Fleshshaper |

- **Decisions (user, 2026-10-30):**
  - `SVY-R25`, the ruling (made during structural review 6): "If you returned something to hand with mode N and then tried to do damage to it with mode N+1, N+1 fizzles." For the knock-on cases, the user chose "Confirm both": an asset that leaves and comes back is a new asset, and a step whose targets are all gone doesn't satisfy "if you do".
  - `SVY-R26`: chose "Presence only" over "Full filter every step".
  - `SVY-R27`: chose "Deals nothing" over "Last-known power".
  - `SVY-R28`: chose "Keep that reading" over "Every mode is legal".
  - `SVY-R29`: chose "Needs two legal modes" for Ambush Tactics, then "Yes, one rule" for every Maneuver with an exactly-one target slot.
  - `SVY-R30`: chose "Keep it" over "Trigger does nothing".
  - `SVY-R31`: chose "Printed order" over "Pick order".
- **Cards affected:**
  - *Ambush Tactics*: wave C, in M2, two copies in the Path starter deck. No text change. With printed order, its kill and return modes run before its damage mode, so "kill or return X" plus "X deals damage to Y" deals nothing (`SVY-R27`). Its put-on-deck mode runs after the damage mode, so "X deals damage to Y, then put X on top of its owner's deck" works.
  - *Rogue Fleshshaper*: wave B, in M2 (Path), already executable. No change, because the engine already behaves as `SVY-R28`, `SVY-R30` and `SVY-R31` say (T-0083).
  - Every Maneuver with a "chosen" target (wave C onward, both starter decks): it can't be enacted without a legal entity for each exactly-one slot (`SVY-R29`). No text changes.
  - *Allshan's Battle Trance* (wave F): its *"It gains Stalwart…"* means the asset it just deployed, which the text names. That asset isn't the old pile target, so `SVY-R25` doesn't touch it, and wave F must not model "It" as the target slot.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Last-known power for a gone dealer:** a dealer removed during resolution would act differently from one removed before resolution, and a target would follow source rules in one place only.
  - **Re-check the full filter at every step:** it adds a third verification point to `FR-TGT-003`, and an earlier step's side effect, such as a move to Reserves, could silently cancel a later step.
  - **Every mode is legal:** it lets a player pick a blank mode, and it makes §6's timeout fallback ("the first listed legal mode(s)") meaningless.
  - **Enact a modal Maneuver with fewer legal modes:** that is the trigger fallback, and a trigger can't be held back. A card in hand can, and allowing it opens a "blank enact into the discard pile" line.
  - **The rule for modal Maneuvers only:** it gives two rules for one question, and plain targeted Maneuvers would keep the blank-enact line.
  - **A modal trigger with too few legal modes does nothing:** it treats triggers like Maneuvers, and it differs from `FR-TGT-003`'s rule for deploy triggers without legal targets.
  - **Pick order:** it adds an ordering decision and a §6 fallback, and changes T-0083's tests and `chooseModes`.

  - **`FR-TGT-003`, after "Partial Invalidation" (line 358):** add two bullets:
    > - **Targets Removed During Resolution (`SVY-R25`, `SVY-R26`)**: A chosen asset that an earlier step or mode of the same effect removed from the battlefield is no longer that target. Each later step does nothing to it and still acts on the rest of its slot (Partial Invalidation, above). An asset that leaves the battlefield and returns during the same resolution is a new asset (`FR-ZON-004`) and is not that target either. Between the two verification stages, a step checks only that each chosen asset is still on the battlefield. It does not re-check the slot's zone, stat or Ward criteria, so an earlier step that moves a target to another battlefield zone or changes its stats does not stop a later step from acting on it. *Example:* *Ambush Tactics* choosing *"Kill chosen unit"* and *"Put chosen unit on top of its owner's deck"* for the same unit kills it. The put-on-deck mode does nothing.
    > - **A Chosen Asset That Deals Damage (`SVY-R27`)**: When a chosen asset deals the damage (*Ambush Tactics*: *"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to another chosen unit"*), it is a target like any other. If it was illegal at the second verification stage, or an earlier step removed it, it deals no damage. Its last-known power is not used, because last-known values are read only for the effect's own source (`SVY-R10`, `FR-ZON-004`). Otherwise it deals damage equal to its current effective power (Layer 6, `FR-LAY-001`) as the step resolves. *Example:* *Ambush Tactics* choosing *"Return chosen unit to its owner's hand"* for unit X and *"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to another chosen unit"* with X dealing: X returns to its owner's hand and deals no damage.

Only legal modes may be chosen. A *"Choose N"* Maneuver can be enacted only if at least N of its modes are legal (`SVY-R29`, `FR-MNV-001`). A triggered ability must still be placed. So *Ambush Tactics*' kill and return modes run before its damage mode, and its put-on-deck mode runs after it.
