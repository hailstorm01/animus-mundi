## 1. Executive Summary & Red-Team Assessment

`01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` (v3.2) provides a clean tabletop-domain rules foundation after relocating data schemas to `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` and runtime memory structs to `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md`. However, cross-referencing the rules against all 167 cards in `data/cards/*.csv` and the companion FRDs exposes **two High-severity resolution ambiguities**, **three Medium-severity conflicts/underdeveloped rules**, and **one Low-severity internal redundancy**:
1. **Ambiguities:** Inter-clause state-based destruction and trigger enqueuing timing during multi-sentence Maneuvers/abilities (*Frostfire Breath*, *MAX-8 Autonomous Warsuit*, *Seismic Instability*), and the boundary between leave-battlefield triggers (`"When this unit is killed"`) versus enter-discard triggers (`"When this card is put into your discard pile"`, *Solarheart Monk*) and self-relocating death triggers (*Unceasing Tide*, *Stormfront of Ideals*).
2. **Conflicts & Underdeveloped Specs:** Misquoted card rules text for *Rage's Wake* and *Allshan's Battle Trance* compared to `data/cards/*.csv` and `reference/02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md`, underspecified interaction between in-resolution `DEPLOY_FREE` effects and alternate-zone static deployment permissions (*Veil Render*), and unspecified snapshot vs. dynamic locking for group-scoped Layer 5 temporary modifiers (*Fire for Effect*, *March of Progress*).
3. **Redundancies:** `FR-CAP-001` (Section 3.3) and `FR-INH-001`–`FR-INH-004` (Section 4.1) duplicate the inherent action rules and timing split.

*(Note: Game and rule balance design questions are excluded per audit scope.)*

## 2. Severity-Ordered Punch-List

### 🔴 Blocker / Critical
_None._

### 🟠 High
- **[DA-RULES-001] [Ambiguity] Inter-Clause State-Based Destruction & Trigger Enqueue Timing in Multi-Step Effects**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-LAY-002`, `FR-TRG-004`, `FR-REP-004`, `FR-TGT-003`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-LAY-002` states that an asset with `Current Health <= 0` or `Effective Maximum Health <= 0` is destroyed *"during the state-based check following effect or combat damage resolution"*, and `FR-TRG-004` states that triggers occurring while a stack item is resolving are placed on top of the stack. However, the specification does not define whether state-based destruction checks execute **between individual sequential clauses/sentences of a single resolving Maneuver or ability** or **only after the entire stack item finishes resolving**, nor when triggers caused by clause 1 are pushed onto the stack relative to clause 2.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. *Intra-Ability Death Check Required (`Frostfire Breath`)*: A player enacts *Frostfire Breath* (`"Deal 2 damage to chosen exhausted non-Scratch asset. If that asset is killed, draw a card."`). For the conditional second sentence to evaluate whether the target was killed (including whether a replacement effect like *Shifting Impossibility* prevented the kill per `FR-REP-001`), state-based destruction must execute immediately after the 2-damage step, prior to evaluating the second sentence.
    2. *Same-Target Damage + Heal (`MAX-8 Autonomous Warsuit`)*: A player deploys *MAX-8 Autonomous Warsuit* (`"When this unit deploys, deal 2 damage to chosen asset. Chosen asset heals 2."`) choosing the same 2-Health allied unit for both clauses (to trigger its `Unbowed` ability and heal it). If state-based destruction runs between sentences, the 2-Health unit is destroyed after Sentence 1 and Sentence 2 (`heals 2`) fizzles.
    3. *Multi-Wave Area Damage (`Seismic Instability`)*: A player enacts *Seismic Instability* (`"Deal 3 damage to all assets in the Main Theater. Deal 4 damage to all structures in the Main Theater."`) against an opponent controlling *Animoceps Spore Cloud* (9 Health Structure) and three allied units. It is undefined whether *Spore Cloud* is destroyed after Sentence 1 (taking $3 + 3 \times 3 = 12$ damage) before Sentence 2 resolves, and whether `EVENT_DESTROY` triggers from Sentence 1 wait until Sentence 2 finishes before enqueuing onto the Resolution Stack.
  - **Remedy:** Specify in `FR-LAY-002` and `FR-TRG-004` that: (1) state-based destruction checks (and inline `WOULD_BE_DESTROYED` replacements) execute immediately after each discrete damage, stat-modification, transformation, or kill step within a resolving ability or Maneuver (so subsequent conditional clauses like *Frostfire Breath* observe the immediate death outcome), while (2) any triggered abilities generated during those steps are buffered and enqueued onto the Resolution Stack in APNAP order only after the currently resolving stack item completes all of its clauses.

- **[DA-RULES-002] [Ambiguity] Leave-Battlefield Death Triggers vs. Enter-Discard Triggers & Self-Relocating Death Triggers**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ZON-004`, `FR-ZON-005`, `FR-ENT-102`, `FR-TRG-006`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Two zone-transition trigger ambiguities exist in Domain 2 and Domain 6:
    1. *Leave-Battlefield (`"When this unit is killed"`) vs. Enter-Discard (`"When this card is put into your discard pile"`)*: `FR-ZON-004` states that abilities triggered by an asset's destruction or departure evaluate that asset's characteristics as they existed on the battlefield immediately prior to departure, while `FR-ZON-005` states that a destroyed Scratch Company turns face-up and immediately reverts to its printed card in Discard, and `FR-ENT-102` notes that tucked cards placed into Discard trigger *Solarheart Monk* (`"When this card is put into your discard pile, chosen asset heals 2"`). It is ambiguous whether a face-down Scratch Company whose underlying card is *Solarheart Monk* triggers *Solarheart Monk* when killed on the battlefield (it had no abilities on the battlefield under `FR-ZON-004`, but enters Discard face-up with its printed abilities under `FR-ZON-005`).
    2. *Self-Relocating Printed Death Triggers vs. Delayed Triggers*: `FR-TRG-006` specifies that delayed triggers (*Rage's Wake*) track the destroyed card in the Discard pile and do nothing if the card leaves the Discard pile before the delayed trigger resolves. However, `FR-ZON-004` does not state whether printed self-relocating death triggers (*Unceasing Tide*: `"When this unit is killed, return it to its owner's hand"`, *Stormfront of Ideals*: `"When this unit is killed, put it on the bottom of its owner's deck"`) similarly track the card in the Discard pile and fizzle if the card already left the Discard pile.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** A player casts *Rage's Wake* on their *Unceasing Tide*, which is killed later that turn. Both *Rage's Wake*'s delayed trigger and *Unceasing Tide*'s printed death trigger enqueue in APNAP order (`FR-TRG-003`). If *Rage's Wake* resolves first and deploys *Unceasing Tide* from Discard to Reserves, and *Unceasing Tide*'s own `"return it to its owner's hand"` trigger resolves second, an implementer cannot determine whether *Unceasing Tide*'s trigger does nothing (because the card left Discard) or bounces the newly deployed battlefield unit from Reserves to Hand.
  - **Remedy:** Clarify in `FR-ZON-004` and `FR-ZON-005` that: (1) `"When this unit/structure is killed"` abilities are **leave-battlefield triggers** evaluated against last-known battlefield state (`FR-ZON-004`, so a face-down Scratch Company does not trigger printed `"When this unit is killed"` abilities), whereas `"When this card is put into your discard pile"` (*Solarheart Monk*, *Eerie Remnant*) are **enter-discard triggers** evaluated against the revealed face-up card's printed characteristics in the Discard pile; and (2) any death or enter-discard trigger that moves `"it"` (the triggering card itself) out of the Discard pile tracks that card in the Discard pile and does nothing if the card has already left the Discard pile prior to resolution.

### 🟡 Medium
- **[DA-RULES-003] [Conflict] Misquoted Rules Text for Rage's Wake and Allshan's Battle Trance vs. Authoritative CSVs**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ZON-005`, `FR-TRN-003a`, `FR-TRG-006`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ZON-005` (line 140) and `FR-TRG-006` (line 311–312) quote *Rage's Wake* as reading `"When that unit is killed this turn, if that card is a unit card, deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free"`, and `FR-TRN-003a` (line 240) quotes *Allshan's Battle Trance* as reading `"Deploy chosen unit card from your discard pile to the Main Theater for free"`. However, the authoritative CSV sheets (`data/cards/maneuvers-concordia.csv` line 11 and `data/cards/maneuvers-path.csv` line 12) and `docs/frd/reference/02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md` (#7 and #11) omit `"if that card is a unit card"` from *Rage's Wake* (and omit a `cardType: "UNIT"` filter from its AST) and omit `"for free"` from *Allshan's Battle Trance*.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Because *Rage's Wake*'s authoritative CSV text and compiled AST (`concordia_rages_wake` in `02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md` lines 288–298) do not contain a `cardType: "UNIT"` filter, relying on card-level text rather than an intrinsic engine rule in `FR-ZON-005` / `FR-TRN-003a` would attempt to deploy a Maneuver or Structure card into Reserves when a Scratch Company dies under *Rage's Wake*.
  - **Remedy:** Synchronize the quoted rules text of *Rage's Wake* and *Allshan's Battle Trance* between `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md`, `data/cards/*.csv`, and `02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md`, and state in `FR-ZON-005` / `FR-TRN-003a` as an intrinsic rules invariant that an effect attempting to deploy a specific card from a non-battlefield zone onto the battlefield checks that the revealed card's printed `Card Type` is legally deployable as that entity type and in that destination zone (leaving non-Unit cards in the Discard pile when a destroyed Scratch Company is redeployed as a unit).

- **[DA-RULES-004] [Underdeveloped Spec] In-Resolution Free Deployment Interaction with Mandatory Additional Costs & Alternate-Zone Static Permissions**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-CST-001`, `FR-TRN-003a`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-CST-001` and `FR-TRN-003a` state that deploying or enacting a card *"for free"* sets its Requisition and Tactics costs to `0` while still requiring any mandatory non-resource additional costs to be paid. However, the specification does not define: (a) whether costs attached to a card's own static alternate-zone deployment permission (*Veil Render*: `"You may deploy this card from your discard pile by killing an allied asset in addition to paying its deployment costs"`) apply when *Veil Render* is deployed from the Discard pile by *another* resolving effect (*Allshan's Battle Trance*, *Mutation Swell*), or (b) what happens if a card chosen during an in-resolution `DEPLOY_FREE` effect has an intrinsic mandatory non-resource additional cost that the player cannot pay.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** A player resolves *Allshan's Battle Trance* targeting *Veil Render* in their Discard pile while controlling 0 battlefield assets. It is ambiguous whether *Veil Render* deploys for free without killing an allied asset (because `"by killing an allied asset"` is the cost of *Veil Render*'s own static permission, not an intrinsic cost of *Allshan's Battle Trance*) or fails to deploy.
  - **Remedy:** Clarify in `FR-CST-001` and `FR-TRN-003a` that: (1) additional costs belonging to a card's static alternate-zone deployment permission (*Veil Render*) apply strictly when the player manually declares a deployment using that static permission (`FR-TRN-003`), not when another resolving effect deploys that card via `DEPLOY_FREE`; and (2) if a card has an intrinsic mandatory non-resource additional deployment cost that cannot be paid during an in-resolution `DEPLOY_FREE`, that card cannot be legally deployed by that effect.

- **[DA-RULES-005] [Ambiguity] Group-Scoped Temporary Modifiers (Layer 5) vs. Subsequent Intra-Turn Zone Movements and Deployments**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-LAY-001`, `FR-LAY-003`, `FR-ZON-004`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Multiple cards apply temporary Layer 5 modifiers (`"until end of turn"`) to a filtered group of assets rather than `"chosen"` targets (e.g., *Fire for Effect*: `"Opposing assets in the Main Theater gain Resistant -1 until end of turn"`, *March of Progress*: `"Allied Mechanized units gain 2 armor and get +2 power until end of turn"`, *Rapturous Sermon*: `"Allied Scratch Company units get +2 power until end of turn"`, *Flickering Ember*: `"T4: Allied assets in the same zone get +1 power until end of turn"`). `FR-LAY-001` and `FR-LAY-003` do not state whether group-scoped Layer 5 modifiers lock onto the matching entities present at the moment the effect resolves or dynamically apply to assets that enter the zone or gain the subtype later in the same turn.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** After *Fire for Effect* resolves, an opposing unit in the Main Theater moves to Reserves, and a new opposing unit marches from Reserves into the Main Theater. Under resolution-time entity locking (`FR-ZON-004`), the unit that moved to Reserves keeps `Resistant -1` and the newly arrived unit is unaffected; under a zone-scoped turn aura, the opposite happens.
  - **Remedy:** Explicitly specify in `FR-LAY-001` and `FR-LAY-003` that one-shot resolving effects that apply Layer 2, Layer 3, or Layer 5 temporary modifiers (`until end of turn` / `until the end of your opponent's turn`) to a filtered group evaluate the filter once at the moment of resolution and attach the modifier instances directly to the matching assets present at that moment.

### 🔵 Low / Clarification
- **[DA-RULES-006] [Redundancy] Duplicate Specification of Inherent Capabilities Between FR-CAP-001 and FR-INH-001–004**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-CAP-001`, `FR-INH-001`, `FR-INH-002`, `FR-INH-003`, `FR-INH-004`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-CAP-001` (Domain 3, Section 3.3) and `FR-INH-001` through `FR-INH-004` (Domain 4, Section 4.1) specify the exact same inherent capability rules twice: upfront readiness checks and exhaustion costs, the timing split between Stack-Pushed Tactical Actions (March, Attack, Deploy Scratch Company requiring an empty stack) and Instantaneous Economic Operations (Resource Generation off-stack), and the per-card-type capability breakdown.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Maintaining duplicate normative rules across Section 3.3 and Section 4.1 creates drift risk whenever inherent action rules are updated.
  - **Remedy:** Consolidate the normative inherent action rules into `FR-CAP-001` and reduce `FR-INH-001`–`FR-INH-004` to a concise summary cross-referencing `FR-CAP-001` (retaining the `FR-INH-001`–`FR-INH-004` identifiers in the heading and traceability matrix).
