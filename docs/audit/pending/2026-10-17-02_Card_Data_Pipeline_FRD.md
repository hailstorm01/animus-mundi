## 1. Executive Summary & Red-Team Assessment
Following the `SOC-002` refactoring that relocated the trigger hook catalog (`FR-TRG-001`), atomic effect primitives (`FR-PRM-001`), bound action payloads (`FR-TGT-101`), and benchmark ASTs from `01` into `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md`, this document now serves as the central modeling bridge between the pure rules (`01`), compiler (`03`), wire protocol (`04`), and headless engine (`05`). However, stress-testing `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` against the 10 authoritative CSV sheets (`data/cards/*.csv`) and the rules in `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` reveals **two critical blockers**: (1) `FR-DAT-006` mandates camelCase CSV column headers that contradict all 10 CSV files and `lint_card_sheets.ps1`, and (2) `FR-ABL-001`–`FR-ABL-007` and `FR-PRM-001` lack schema definitions and primitives for several canonical mechanics present in the 167-card set (hand-active cost reductions, discard-pile deployment permissions, delayed triggers, allied-unit exhaustion costs, optional trigger costs, delegated damage sources, and forced free-deploy zones).

## 2. Severity-Ordered Punch-List

### 🔴 Blocker / Critical
- **[DA-DAT-001] `FR-DAT-006` CSV Column Header Contract Contradicts All 10 Authoritative CSV Sheets & Linter**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-DAT-001`, `FR-DAT-006`, `FR-DAT-007`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-DAT-006` (line 172) mandates that *"all authoring CSV files in `data/cards/` use column headers that directly mirror canonical Blueprint JSON property names (`name`, `cardType`, `faction`, `subtypes`, `requisitionCost`, `tacticsCost`, `deploymentZones`, `rulesText`, `power`, `health`, `requisitionYield`, `tacticsYield`, `rarity`, `currentProxy`, `needsRework`)."* In reality, all 10 authoritative CSV files in `data/cards/` (and `tools/linter/lint_card_sheets.ps1`) use human-readable Title Case headers:
    - Unit/Structure/HQ CSVs (15 columns): `"Name","Card Type","Faction","Types","Requisition Cost","Tactics Cost","Deployment Zones","Text","Power","Health","Requisition Value","Tactics Value","Rarity","Current Proxy?","Needs Rework?"`
    - Maneuver CSVs (9 columns): `"Name","Card Type","Faction","Requisition Cost","Tactics Cost","Text","Rarity","Current Proxy?","Needs Rework?"` (omitting `Types`, `Deployment Zones`, `Power`, `Health`, `Requisition Value`, and `Tactics Value`).
    - Boolean columns (`Current Proxy?`, `Needs Rework?`) serialize `"Yes"` / `"No"` rather than `"TRUE"` / `"FALSE"`.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. An engineer implements `compile_blueprints.ps1` (`FR-CMP-004`) to require the 15 camelCase column headers mandated by `FR-DAT-006`.
    2. Running the compiler against `data/cards/*.csv` immediately fails with `ERR_CSV_MALFORMED` on all 10 CSV files because not a single CSV file in the repository uses camelCase headers or includes 15 columns on Maneuver sheets.
  - **Remedy:** Update `FR-DAT-006` (and `03_Card_Compiler_FRD.md` `FR-CMP-004`/`FR-CMP-005`) to specify the exact 15-column Unit/Structure/HQ header schema and 9-column Maneuver header schema (with `"Yes"`/`"No"` boolean flags) currently used in `data/cards/*.csv` and validated by `tools/linter/lint_card_sheets.ps1`, documenting the deterministic mapping from those CSV column headers to the camelCase `CardBlueprint` JSON properties in `FR-DAT-005`.

- **[DA-DAT-002] Missing AST Archetypes, Cost Types & Atomic Primitives for Canonical Card Mechanics**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ABL-001`, `FR-ABL-002`, `FR-ABL-004`, `FR-ABL-007`, `FR-PRM-001`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Multiple canonical game rules in `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` and cards in `data/cards/*.csv` have no corresponding representation in `FR-ABL-001`–`FR-ABL-007` or the 25 atomic primitives of `FR-PRM-001`:
    1. **Self Cost-Reduction Modifiers in Hand (`FR-CST-001` — *Swirling Dustdevil*, *Boneyard Amalgam*)**: *Swirling Dustdevil* (*"This unit costs R1 less to deploy for each maneuver card in your discard pile."*) and *Boneyard Amalgam* (*"This unit costs R1 less to deploy for each unit card in your discard pile."*) modify deployment cost while in Hand. Yet `ContinuousAuraDef` (`FR-ABL-004`) only evaluates while the host is on the battlefield (`FR-ENG-023`), and `AttributeModifierDef` (`FR-ABL-004` line 276) only supports `COMBAT_POWER`, `MAX_HEALTH`, `KEYWORD_GRANT`, and `SUBTYPE_GRANT`—omitting `REQUISITION_COST` and `TACTICS_COST`.
    2. **Alternate-Zone Static Deployment Permission (`FR-TRN-003a` — *Veil Render*)**: *Veil Render* (*"You may deploy this card from your discard pile by killing an allied asset in addition to paying its deployment costs."*) has no representation in `CardBlueprint` (`FR-DAT-005`) or `AbilityDef` (`FR-ABL-001`).
    3. **Delayed Triggered Abilities (*Rage's Wake*, *Redline Infusion*)**: `FR-PRM-001` lacks a `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` primitive to register turn-scoped triggers (*"When that unit is killed this turn, deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free"* on *Rage's Wake*; *"At the end of your turn, kill that unit"* on *Redline Infusion*).
    4. **Additional Enactment Exhaust Cost (*Funeral Fungus*)**: `CostDef.costType` (`FR-ABL-007` line 341) enumerates `["RESOURCE", "EXHAUST_SELF", "KILL_ASSET", "DISCARD_CARD", "SACRIFICE_ALLY"]`, omitting `EXHAUST_ALLY` / `EXHAUST_ASSET` required by *Funeral Fungus* (*"As an additional cost to enact this maneuver, exhaust a readied allied unit."*) and `TARGET_ROLE_EXHAUST_COST` (`04_Client_Engine_Protocol_Networking_FRD.md` line 390).
    5. **Optional Non-Resource Trigger Costs / `"You May ... If You Do"` Clauses (*Blind Defile*, *Infested Apostle*, *Arkha, Sun's Fury*, *Redlined Brawler*)**: `TriggeredAbilityDef` (`FR-ABL-002`) has no `optionalCost` / `costs` property, and `COMPOSITE_SEQUENCE` / `CONDITIONAL_CLAUSE` (referenced in `FR-PRM-001` prose and used in the *Samsara* benchmark on lines 608 and 621) have no formal JSON schema definition in `FR-ABL-007` or `FR-PRM-001`.
    6. **Delegated Damage Source (*Soul Whip*, *Ambush Tactics*) & Forced Free-Deploy Zone (*Allshan's Battle Trance*, *Rage's Wake*)**: `DEAL_DAMAGE` (`FR-PRM-001` line 387) has only `amount: ValueToken` (missing `damageSource: TargetRef` when a chosen unit deals damage equal to its power), and `DEPLOY_FREE` (line 399) has only `sourceZone: Enum, filter: FilterDef` (missing `destinationZone: Enum`).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Attempting to author or compile `blueprints.json` for the 167 cards in `data/cards/*.csv` fails because at least 13 canonical cards cannot be expressed within the schemas of `FR-ABL-001`–`FR-ABL-007` and `FR-PRM-001`.
  - **Remedy:** Expand `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` to define: (1) `REQUISITION_COST` and `TACTICS_COST` in `AttributeModifierDef` (`FR-ABL-004`) plus an `activeZone` property (`BATTLEFIELD` default, `HAND`, or `DISCARD`) or a dedicated `COST_MODIFIER` / `STATIC_PERMISSION` schema for *Swirling Dustdevil*, *Boneyard Amalgam*, and *Veil Render*; (2) `EXHAUST_ALLY` in `CostDef.costType` (`FR-ABL-007`); (3) `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` in `FR-PRM-001`; (4) formal schemas for `COMPOSITE_SEQUENCE` and `CONDITIONAL_CLAUSE` (including optional `"you may"` cost/action gates for triggers) in `FR-ABL-007` / `FR-PRM-001`; and (5) optional `damageSource: TargetRef` on `DEAL_DAMAGE` and `destinationZone: Enum` on `DEPLOY_FREE` in `FR-PRM-001`.

### 🟠 High
- **[DA-DAT-003] `FilterDef` and `TargetRef` (`FR-ABL-007`) Omit Stat/Cost Comparisons, `excludeSelf`, Quantifier Bounds, and `BOUND_TARGET`**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ABL-007`, `FR-TGT-101`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:**
    1. `FilterDef` (`FR-ABL-007`, lines 301–315) omits stat and cost comparison predicates (`statComparison: { property, operator, value: ValueToken }`) required by `FR-TGT-001` and cards such as *Consuming Earth* (`power <= total structure Requisition yield`), *Gemstone Titan* (`current Health < this unit's power`), *Will Breaker* / *Devilhorn Scouting Party* (`Requisition cost <= 3`), and *Reaver's Rush* (`power <= 3`).
    2. `FilterDef` omits `excludeSelf: boolean`, which is required by *Craterfall Ancient* (*"up to 2 other chosen assets"*), *Tempest Runner* (*"another chosen allied unit"*), *Dread Manifestation* (*"another unit"*), *Animoceps Stalk*, and *Ancestral Monument* (and is emitted by `03_Card_Compiler_FRD.md` `FR-CMP-015` line 363).
    3. `TargetRef` (`FR-ABL-007`, lines 348–356) defines only `count: integer (minimum: 1)`, lacking `minCount` / `maxCount` (for *"up to N chosen"*) or `allowedCounts` (for *"2 or 4 allied Scratch Company units"* on *Mutation Swell*), and omits `selector: "BOUND_TARGET"`, `targetIndex`, and `bindingPolicy: "UPFRONT_LOCK"` used directly in Section 4.2 Benchmark #4 (*Samsara, Living Tempest*, lines 605 and 628).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Section 4.2 Benchmark #4 (*Samsara, Living Tempest*) inside `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` fails JSON schema validation against `TargetRef` (`FR-ABL-007`) in the very same document.
  - **Remedy:** Update `FilterDef` and `TargetRef` in `FR-ABL-007` to include `statComparison`, `excludeSelf`, `minCount` / `maxCount` / `allowedCounts`, `bindingPolicy: ["UPFRONT_LOCK", "ON_RESOLUTION"]`, and `selector: "BOUND_TARGET"` (with `targetIndex: integer`).

- **[DA-DAT-004] Contradiction Between `FR-ABL-001` `abilityType` Enum and `FR-ABL-012` Keyword AST Archetype Table**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ABL-001`, `FR-ABL-012`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ABL-001` restricts `AbilityDef.abilityType` to 6 enum values (`TRIGGERED`, `ACTIVATED`, `CONTINUOUS_AURA`, `REPLACEMENT_WATCHER`, `MANEUVER_EFFECT`, `KEYWORD_GRANT`), and Section 4.2 Benchmarks #1 and #2 serialize keywords using `{ "abilityType": "KEYWORD_GRANT", "keyword": "WARD" }` and `{ "abilityType": "KEYWORD_GRANT", "keyword": "AGGRESSIVE", "parameter": 9 }`. However, `FR-ABL-012` (lines 448–464) defines a column titled `AST Schema Archetype` with values (`STATIC_RESTRICTION`, `CAPABILITY_FLAG`, `STATIC_ATTRIBUTE`, `CONDITIONAL_GATE`, `ATOMIC_PRIMITIVE`) that do not exist in `FR-ABL-001`.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** The Card Compiler (`FR-CMP-013` in `03_Card_Compiler_FRD.md`) references `FR-ABL-012` to synthesize keyword AST nodes; emitting `abilityType: "STATIC_RESTRICTION"` or `"CAPABILITY_FLAG"` fails schema validation against `FR-ABL-001` and fails the Go type-switch visitor in `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` (`FR-ENG-018`).
  - **Remedy:** Align `FR-ABL-012` with `FR-ABL-001` and Section 4.2 by clarifying that standalone printed asset keywords serialize in JSON with `abilityType: "KEYWORD_GRANT"` (while keyword actions like `Heal N`, `Scratch N`, and `Regroup N` compile into effect primitives inside `TRIGGERED`, `ACTIVATED`, or `MANEUVER_EFFECT` abilities), and rename the column in `FR-ABL-012` to `Engine Execution Semantics`.

### 🟡 Medium
- **[DA-DAT-005] Card Text & Stat Drift Between Section 4.2 / Section 7.2 of `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` and `data/cards/*.csv`**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-DAT-001`, `FR-DAT-005`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Several cards documented in Section 4.2 (Benchmark Implementations) and Section 7.2 (Master Review Register) of `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` do not match the actual Tier 1 CSV files in `data/cards/*.csv`:
    1. *Piston Fist Prototype*: Section 4.2 (lines 520–548) and Section 7.2 (line 816) specify `3R/3T`, `1 Power / 9 Health`, and `rulesText: "Aggressive 9\nAfter this unit attacks, move this unit to your Reserves."`, whereas `units-and-structures-soulless.csv` line 24 has `4R/4T`, `9 Power / 8 Health`, and `Text: "After this unit attacks, move this unit to your Reserves."`.
    2. *Hammerhand Bivouac*: Section 7.2 (line 808) lists `"Distribute 2 armor among allied units in the same zone."` (missing `"At the start of your turn, "` and listing 2 armor instead of 1), whereas `units-and-structures-path.csv` line 17 has `"At the start of your turn, distribute 1 armor among allied units in the same zone."`.
    3. *Eerie Remnant*: Section 7.2 (line 792) lists `"When this card is put into your discard pile from your hand, you may deploy it from your discard pile for free."`, whereas `units-and-structures-path.csv` line 5 still has `"When this card is put into your discard pile from your hand, you may deploy it for free instead."`.
    4. *Ambush Tactics* & *Gemstone Titan*: Section 7.2 (lines 772, 818) specifies `"another chosen unit"` and `"chosen opposing structure"`, whereas `maneuvers-path.csv` line 16 and `units-and-structures-concordia.csv` line 26 have `"chosen unit"` and `"chosen structure"`.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Under `FR-CMP-017` (Desynchronization Check), any sidecar override in `ast_overrides.json` authored from Section 4.2 or Section 7.2 halts compilation with `ERR_OVERRIDE_TEXT_MISMATCH`.
  - **Remedy:** Reconcile `data/cards/*.csv` and `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (Sections 4.2 and 7.2) so that all card stats and `rulesText` strings are 100% identical.

- **[DA-DAT-006] Ambiguous Duplicate Turn/Phase Event Hooks & Missing `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` Filter Schema in `FR-TRG-001`**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-TRG-001`, `FR-ABL-002`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-TRG-001` (line 376) lists both `EVENT_READY_PHASE` and `EVENT_TURN_START`, and both `EVENT_END_PHASE` and `EVENT_TURN_END`, without specifying which hook is bound by `"At the start of your turn"` (*Hammerhand Bivouac*) and `"At the end of your turn"` (*Redline Infusion*). Additionally, `FR-TRG-001` lists `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` without defining the `ConditionDef` properties that distinguish attacker-only combat kills (*Eternal Watchman*) from any-combatant combat kills (*Dread Manifestation*).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** If the compiler binds start-of-turn triggers to `EVENT_TURN_START` while an engine sub-phase emits only `EVENT_READY_PHASE` (or emits both and triggers fire twice), turn-boundary triggers break.
  - **Remedy:** Specify in `FR-TRG-001` that `EVENT_TURN_START` (emitted in Sub-Phase 1b of the Ready Phase) and `EVENT_TURN_END` (emitted in Sub-Phase 4a of the End Phase) are the canonical hooks for `"At the start of your turn"` and `"At the end of your turn"`, and document the `ConditionDef` schema (`source`, `turnOwner`, `cornered`, `combatRole`) in `FR-ABL-007`.
