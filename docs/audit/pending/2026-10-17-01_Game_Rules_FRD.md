## 1. Executive Summary & Red-Team Assessment
Following the `SOC-002` pure-rules refactoring (`v3.0 -> v3.1`), `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` cleanly separates tabletop game rules from software data schemas. However, cross-referencing the pure rules against the 167 canonical cards in `data/cards/*.csv` and the downstream data/engine specifications (`02` and `05`) exposes a critical rules gap around **delayed triggered abilities** and **post-death Scratch Company re-deployment** (*Rage's Wake*, *Redline Infusion*), high-severity ambiguities in **in-resolution free deployment zone restrictions** (*Allshan's Battle Trance*, *Rage's Wake*) and **non-battlefield static deployment permissions** (*Veil Render*), and factual misattributions of canonical cards (*Portent Scrivener*, *Eerie Remnant*).

## 2. Severity-Ordered Punch-List

### 🔴 Blocker / Critical
- **[DA-RULES-001] Missing Delayed Triggered Ability Rules & Post-Death Scratch Company Re-Deployment Paradox (*Rage's Wake*, *Redline Infusion*)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-TRN-001`, `FR-TRN-003a`, `FR-ZON-004`, `FR-ZON-005`, `FR-TRG-003`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` defines triggered abilities printed on battlefield assets and immediate zone-entry triggers (*Solarheart Monk*, *Eerie Remnant*), but never defines **delayed triggered abilities** created dynamically by resolving Maneuvers (*Rage's Wake*: *"Chosen unit gets +2 power until end of turn. When that unit is killed this turn, deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free."*; *Redline Infusion*: *"Chosen allied unit gets +2 power and gains Berserk until end of turn. At the end of your turn, kill that unit."*). Furthermore, `FR-ZON-004` strips all temporary modifiers when an asset leaves the battlefield, and `FR-ZON-005` mandates that when a face-down **Scratch Company** unit is destroyed and enters the Discard pile, it turns face-up and reverts to its printed card characteristics.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. Player A enacts *Rage's Wake* targeting an allied face-down **Scratch Company** unit (`1/1 Infantry Scratch Company`).
    2. Later that turn, that Scratch Company unit is killed in combat and moves to Player A's Discard pile, turning face-up (`FR-ZON-005`) and reverting to its printed card (`FR-ZON-004`).
    3. If the underlying card of that Scratch Company is a **Maneuver card** (e.g., *Growing Legion*) or a **Structure card**, *Rage's Wake* instructs Player A to *"deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free."* A Maneuver card cannot exist on the battlefield (`FR-CRD-101`, `FR-MNV-001`), creating an illegal rules state.
    4. Even for a standard Unit card, `FR-ZON-004` strips all temporary modifiers upon leaving the battlefield, leaving it undefined how *"When that unit is killed this turn"* survives the unit's death and tracks the resulting card into the Discard pile.
  - **Remedy:** Add a **Delayed Triggered Abilities** rule to Domain 6 (Section 6.1) specifying that a resolving effect may register a turn-scoped delayed trigger bound to a specific entity and event (`When that unit is killed this turn` or `At the end of your turn`), and specify in `FR-TRN-003a` / `FR-ZON-005` that if an effect attempts to deploy a specific card from a non-battlefield zone whose printed Card Type in that zone is not a legal battlefield asset (or does not match the effect's card type constraint, such as a Scratch Company that reverted to a Maneuver card in Discard), the deployment does not occur and the card remains in its current zone.

### 🟠 High
- **[DA-RULES-002] In-Resolution Free Deployment Destination Zone vs. Printed `Deployment Zones` Conflict (*Allshan's Battle Trance*, *Rage's Wake*, *Mutation Swell*)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-CRD-101`, `FR-TRN-003`, `FR-TRN-003a`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-CRD-101` and `FR-TRN-003` state that every Unit and Structure has printed `Deployment Zones` (`Reserves`, `Main Theater`, or both) governing where it may legally be deployed. However, `FR-TRN-003a` (In-Resolution Free Deployments) fails to specify: (1) whether effects that explicitly mandate a destination zone (*Allshan's Battle Trance*: *"Deploy chosen unit card from your discard pile to the Main Theater..."*; *Rage's Wake*: *"...deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free"*) override the card's printed `Deployment Zones` (23 of 28 Path units have only `RESERVES` as their printed deployment zone, while units like *Brigand Highlander* and *Samsara, Living Tempest* have only `MAIN_THEATER`), and (2) how the destination zone is determined when a free-deployment effect does *not* name a zone (*Mutation Swell*, *World Soul Messenger*, *Light of the World Soul*, *Devilhorn Scouting Party*, *Reaver's Rush*, *Eerie Remnant*). Additionally, `FR-TRN-003a` cites *Allshan's Battle Trance* as deploying *"for free"*, whereas its CSV text in `maneuvers-path.csv` omits the words *"for free"*.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. Player A enacts *Allshan's Battle Trance* targeting *Boneyard Amalgam* (`deploymentZones: ["RESERVES"]`) in their Discard pile, or *Rage's Wake* triggers when *Samsara, Living Tempest* (`deploymentZones: ["MAIN_THEATER"]`) is killed.
    2. Under a strict reading of `FR-CRD-101`, deploying *Boneyard Amalgam* to the Main Theater or *Samsara* to Reserves is illegal; under an override reading, explicit zone-specifying effects bypass printed `Deployment Zones`.
    3. When *World Soul Messenger* deploys a multi-zone unit (*Tempest Runner*, `deploymentZones: ["MAIN_THEATER", "RESERVES"]`) *"for free"*, `FR-TRN-003a` does not specify whether the controller chooses the destination zone.
  - **Remedy:** Specify in `FR-TRN-003a` that: (1) when an in-resolution deployment does not name a destination zone, the card is deployed into one of its printed `Deployment Zones` (chosen by its controller if both `Reserves` and `Main Theater` are printed); (2) when an effect explicitly names a destination zone (*"to the Main Theater"* or *"to your Reserves"*), that effect overrides the card's printed `Deployment Zones`; and (3) synchronize *Allshan's Battle Trance*'s rules text to include *"for free"*.

- **[DA-RULES-003] Missing Non-Battlefield Static Deployment Permissions & On-Stack Zone Definition (*Veil Render*)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ZON-001`, `FR-TRN-003`, `FR-TRN-003a`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-TRN-003` specifies that manual card deployments and Maneuver enactments occur strictly *"from Hand"*, while `FR-TRN-003a` covers only additional costs and in-resolution *"for free"* deployments. However, *Veil Render* (`"You may deploy this card from your discard pile by killing an allied asset in addition to paying its deployment costs."`) grants a static permission to manually declare a paid deployment onto the Resolution Stack directly from the **Discard** pile. Moreover, `FR-ZON-001` omits the **Resolution Stack** from the zone definitions (whereas `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` `FR-ENT-002` assigns `CurrentZone = STACK` while a card is on the stack).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. Player A declares the deployment of *Veil Render* from their Discard pile, sacrificing an allied unit with a death trigger (*Unceasing Tide* or *Boneyard Scavenger*) as the additional cost (`FR-TRN-003a`).
    2. While *Veil Render* is on the Resolution Stack awaiting resolution, if it is not in a distinct `Stack` zone (`FR-ZON-001`), effects or triggers inspecting the Discard pile can still target or count *Veil Render* in the Discard pile.
  - **Remedy:** Add the **Resolution Stack** to `FR-ZON-001` as the zone occupied by Cards while their deployment or enactment is on the stack, and expand `FR-TRN-003` / `FR-TRN-003a` to define manual stack-pushed deployments from the Discard pile when permitted by a card's static ability (*Veil Render*).

### 🟡 Medium
- **[DA-RULES-004] Factual Misattribution of *Portent Scrivener* & *Eerie Remnant* in `FR-TRN-001` and `FR-ENT-102`**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-TRN-001`, `FR-ENT-102`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Two card citations in `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` directly contradict the actual printed rules text of those cards in `data/cards/`:
    1. `FR-TRN-001` Phase 2 (Draw Phase, line 179) and Revision Summary item 2 (line 19) cite *Portent Scrivener* as an ability triggered by the Draw Phase card draw (*"Any abilities triggered by that card draw (Portent Scrivener)..."*). In `units-and-structures-animus.csv`, *Portent Scrivener*'s text is `"When this unit is killed on your turn, draw a card."`—an `EVENT_DESTROY` trigger conditioned on active-turn context, not a Draw Phase trigger.
    2. `FR-ENT-102` (line 144) states that when a host asset leaves the battlefield and its tucked cards are placed into the Discard pile, *"Placing tucked cards into the Discard pile this way triggers any abilities that watch for cards entering a Discard pile (Solarheart Monk, Eerie Remnant)..."* In `units-and-structures-path.csv`, *Eerie Remnant* triggers strictly *"When this card is put into your discard pile from your hand"*, so moving it from an `Attached` sub-zone to the Discard pile must **not** trigger *Eerie Remnant*.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Engine engineers implementing `FR-ENT-102` will erroneously fire *Eerie Remnant* when tucked cards are purged from *NO-AH-5*, and engineers implementing `FR-TRN-001` will bind *Portent Scrivener* to `EVENT_DRAW_CARD`.
  - **Remedy:** Remove the *Portent Scrivener* citation from `FR-TRN-001` Phase 2 (and Revision Summary item 2), and remove *Eerie Remnant* from `FR-ENT-102` (leaving *Solarheart Monk*, which triggers when put into the discard pile from any zone).

- **[DA-RULES-005] Undefined Trigger Conditions for *"Deals Damage"* (*Tidal Leviathan*) and *"Kills in Combat"* (*Dread Manifestation* vs. *Eternal Watchman*)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-REP-004`, `FR-CBT-002`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** While `FR-REP-004` Stage 5 defines when an asset **is dealt / takes damage** (`(armorAbsorbed + healthDamageCommitted) > 0`), it never defines the mirror condition for when an asset **deals damage** (*Tidal Leviathan*: *"Whenever this Headquarters deals damage, chosen allied unit or structure heals 1."*). Similarly, `FR-CBT-002` Step 3 mentions *"combat-kill abilities"* without distinguishing *"Whenever this Headquarters attacks and kills an opposing unit or structure"* (*Eternal Watchman*, attacker-only) from *"Whenever this unit kills another unit in combat"* (*Dread Manifestation*, which can occur either when attacking or via symmetric retaliation when defending).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. *Tidal Leviathan* (`3 Power`) attacks an opposing unit with `Resistant 3` (reduced to 0 in Stage 2) or `Armor 2` (absorbed in Stage 4), or its damage is redirected to *Animoceps Spore Cloud* in Stage 1. Without an explicit rule in `FR-REP-004`, it is ambiguous whether *Tidal Leviathan*'s trigger fires when damage is redirected or absorbed by Armor.
    2. An opposing unit attacks *Dread Manifestation* and is destroyed by *Dread Manifestation*'s symmetric retaliation damage in Step 3 of `FR-CBT-002`. It is unspecified whether killing an attacker via retaliation triggers *"Whenever this unit kills another unit in combat"*.
  - **Remedy:** Specify in `FR-REP-004` Stage 5 that an asset is considered to have **dealt damage** if and only if at least 1 point of damage from that asset is absorbed by Armor in Stage 4 or committed to Health in Stage 5 (`(armorAbsorbed + healthDamageCommitted) > 0`), regardless of Stage 1 target redirection. Specify in `FR-CBT-002` Step 3 that *"kills [an entity] in combat"* (*Dread Manifestation*) triggers whenever the asset's combat strike or symmetric retaliation damage causes lethal destruction in Step 3, whereas *"attacks and kills"* (*Eternal Watchman*) requires the asset to be the declared attacker.

### 🔵 Low / Clarification
- **[DA-RULES-006] Non-Combat Delegated Unit Damage (`Soul Whip`, `Ambush Tactics`) vs. `Stalwart` & Damage Triggers**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-REP-004`, `FR-KWD-002c`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-REP-004` Stage 3 states that **Stalwart** applies *"only if the damage is combat damage (from an Attack or combat retaliation; `FR-CBT-002`)"*. Cards like *Soul Whip* (*"Chosen allied unit deals damage equal to its power to chosen opposing unit."*) and *Ambush Tactics* Mode 3 (*"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power to chosen unit."*) instruct a unit to deal damage equal to its Combat Power outside of an Attack. While `FR-REP-004` implies this is non-combat effect damage (bypassing Stalwart), explicitly stating that unit-sourced damage instructed by a Maneuver or ability is non-combat effect damage prevents confusion.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Players or implementers may conflate *"Chosen unit deals damage equal to its power"* with combat damage because a unit is the damage source and uses its Combat Power.
  - **Remedy:** Add a one-sentence clarification to `FR-REP-004` Stage 3 (`Stalwart`) confirming that damage dealt by a unit via a Maneuver or ability (*Soul Whip*, *Ambush Tactics*) is non-combat effect damage and does not trigger Stage 3 Stalwart capping or symmetric retaliation.

1. *When a player enacts a card like Allshan's Battle Trance ("Deploy chosen unit card from your discard pile to the Main Theater") or triggers Rage's Wake ("deploy it from your discard pile to your Reserves for free"), is that explicit destination zone intended to override the unit's printed `deploymentZones` (allowing a Reserves-only unit to enter the Main Theater via Allshan's Battle Trance, or a Main-Theater-only unit like Samsara to enter Reserves via Rage's Wake), or should printed `deploymentZones` restrict target/re-deployment eligibility?*
