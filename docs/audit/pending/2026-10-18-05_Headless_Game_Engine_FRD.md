## 1. Executive Summary & Red-Team Assessment

`05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` (v1.2) provides a well-structured deterministic Go runtime specification with a 3-tier entity memory model, two-phase choice transaction boundaries, and turn-scoped delayed trigger tracking. However, cross-referencing its execution contracts against `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md`, `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md`, and `04_Client_Engine_Protocol_Networking_FRD.md` uncovers **one Blocker-severity cross-FRD schema conflict/gap**, **two High-severity rules/event execution conflicts**, and **one Medium-severity state-based sweep ambiguity**:
1. **Blocker Cross-FRD Schema Conflicts (`DA-ENG-001`):** `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` (Executive Summary, Architecture Diagrams, and `FR-ENG-021`) specifies **25** atomic effect primitives and omits `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` (#26 in `FR-PRM-001` of `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md`), which under `FR-ENG-021`'s safety invariant causes any card using `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` (*Rage's Wake*, *Redline Infusion*) to abort with `ERR_INVALID_COMMAND_ARGUMENTS`. Additionally, `FR-ENG-013` checks for `costType: "EXHAUST_ALLY"` instead of `FR-ABL-007`'s `"EXHAUST_ASSET"` (*Funeral Fungus*), and `FR-ENG-013` / `FR-ENG-025` check for a non-existent `COST_MODIFIER` query instead of `CONTINUOUS_AURA` with `activeZone: "HAND" | "DISCARD"` (`FR-ABL-004`), while `FR-ENG-023` restricts `CONTINUOUS_AURA` strictly to battlefield `AssetInstance`s.
2. **High Event & Zone Execution Conflicts (`DA-ENG-002`, `DA-ENG-003`):** `FR-ENG-034` omits emitting `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE` in Stage 5 of damage mitigation (breaking *Tidal Leviathan* and delegated damage sources like *Soul Whip*), while `FR-ENG-035` emits `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` whenever *any* asset is destroyed by combat damage (violating the attacker-only invariant of `FR-CBT-002` and `FR-TRG-001` on mutual combat deaths); `FR-ENG-006`, `FR-ENG-012`, `FR-ENG-013`, and `FR-ENG-024` fail to transition a declared card's `CardInstance.CurrentZone` from `HAND` or `DISCARD` to `STACK` upon declaration prior to paying additional costs or resolving effects (violating `FR-ZON-001` and `FR-TRN-003` on *Steel Rain* and *Veil Render*).
3. **Medium State-Based Sweep Ambiguity (`DA-ENG-004`):** `FR-ENG-035` triggers the state-based death sweep only *"Immediately following damage commitment"*, omitting non-damage lethal state changes (`MODIFY_STAT` reducing `EffectiveMaxHealth <= 0` on *Poisoned Rations*, aura source departure in `FR-ENG-023`, and `MORPH_SCRATCH` on a damaged unit via *Forced Mutation*), and leaves inter-step death sweep vs. trigger enqueuing inside `COMPOSITE_SEQUENCE` underspecified.

*(Note: Game and rule balance design questions are excluded per audit scope.)*

## 2. Severity-Ordered Punch-List

### 🔴 Blocker / Critical
- **[DA-ENG-001] [Conflict / Gap] Engine Specifies 25 Atomic Primitives (Omitting REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER) and Uses Conflicting AST Identifiers (EXHAUST_ALLY, COST_MODIFIER) vs. FRD 02**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-013`, `FR-ENG-018`, `FR-ENG-021`, `FR-ENG-023`, `FR-ENG-025`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** Three direct cross-FRD schema conflicts exist between `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` and `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md`:
    1. *25 vs. 26 Atomic Primitives (`REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` Omitted)*: `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (`FR-PRM-001`) defines **26** canonical atomic effect primitives, with #26 being `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` (*Rage's Wake*, *Redline Infusion*). However, `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` (Executive Summary line 12, Architecture Diagram line 39, Domain 4 Diagram lines 458–466, and `FR-ENG-021` line 481) specifies **25** atomic effect primitives and omits `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` from the interpreter catalog. Because `FR-ENG-021` mandates that any unrecognized primitive aborts the transaction with `ERR_INVALID_COMMAND_ARGUMENTS`, `MatchState.DelayedTriggers` (`FR-ENG-006`) can never be populated by `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER`.
    2. *`EXHAUST_ALLY` vs. `EXHAUST_ASSET`*: `FR-ENG-013` (line 409) and the Revision Summary (line 58) specify `costType: "EXHAUST_ALLY"` for allied unit exhaustion costs (*Funeral Fungus*), whereas `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (`FR-ABL-007` line 224) and `reference/02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md` (#8 *Funeral Fungus* line 319) serialize `costType: "EXHAUST_ASSET"`.
    3. *`COST_MODIFIER` vs. `CONTINUOUS_AURA` (`activeZone: "HAND" | "DISCARD"`)*: `FR-ENG-013` (line 407) and `FR-ENG-025` (line 500) instruct the engine to evaluate `COST_MODIFIER` queries on `CardInstance` structs in `HandZone` or `DiscardZone` (*Swirling Dustdevil*, *Boneyard Amalgam*), while `FR-ENG-023` (line 488) states that `CONTINUOUS_AURA` only applies while the host `AssetInstance` occupies a battlefield zone. In `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (`FR-ABL-004` lines 195–200) and `02_Benchmark_Card_Blueprints.md` (#5 *Swirling Dustdevil*, #6 *Veil Render*), hand cost reductions and discard deployment permissions are modeled as `abilityType: "CONTINUOUS_AURA"` with `activeZone: "HAND"` or `"DISCARD"`—there is no `COST_MODIFIER` node in `FR-ABL-001`.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Executing `blueprints.json` compiled per FRD 02 in an engine implemented per FRD 05 aborts with `ERR_INVALID_COMMAND_ARGUMENTS` on *Rage's Wake* and *Redline Infusion* (`REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER`), fails to match `EXHAUST_ASSET` on *Funeral Fungus*, and ignores `CONTINUOUS_AURA` nodes with `activeZone: "HAND"` or `"DISCARD"` on *Swirling Dustdevil*, *Boneyard Amalgam*, and *Veil Render*.
  - **Remedy:** Update `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` (lines 12, 39, 458–466, and `FR-ENG-021`) to reference all **26** canonical atomic effect primitives in `FR-PRM-001` (adding `REGISTER_DELAYED_TRIGGER` as the handler that appends to `MatchState.DelayedTriggers`), change `EXHAUST_ALLY` to `EXHAUST_ASSET` (and distinguish `KILL_SELF` vs. `KILL_ASSET`) in `FR-ENG-013`, and update `FR-ENG-013`, `FR-ENG-023`, and `FR-ENG-025` to evaluate `CONTINUOUS_AURA` nodes with `activeZone: "HAND"` or `"DISCARD"` per `FR-ABL-004`.

### 🟠 High
- **[DA-ENG-002] [Gap / Conflict] Missing EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE Emission in FR-ENG-034 and Over-Broad EVENT_COMBAT_KILL Emission in FR-ENG-035**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-034`, `FR-ENG-035`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:**
    1. *Missing `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE` in Stage 5 (`FR-ENG-034`)*: `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` (`FR-REP-004` Stage 5, line 393) and `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (`FR-TRG-001`) mandate that when `(armorAbsorbed + healthDamageCommitted) > 0` on the final post-Stage-1 target, the source asset (including delegated damage sources specified via `DEAL_DAMAGE.damageSource` on *Soul Whip* and *Ambush Tactics*) deals damage, emitting `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE` (required by *Tidal Leviathan*: `"Whenever this Headquarters deals damage, chosen allied unit or structure heals 1"`). However, `FR-ENG-034` (line 535) only specifies emitting `EVENT_TAKE_DAMAGE` and completely omits `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE`.
    2. *Over-Broad `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` in `FR-ENG-035`*: `FR-ENG-035` bullet 2 (line 540) states that for each destroyed asset, the engine *"emits `EVENT_DESTROY` (and `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` if destroyed by combat damage)"*. This conflicts with `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` (`FR-CBT-002` line 431) and `02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md` (`FR-TRG-001` line 240), which mandate that `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` is emitted **strictly when the declared attacker's combat damage destroys the defending asset**, and **never** when a defender's retaliation damage destroys the attacker.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** *Tidal Leviathan* and any other `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE` triggers never fire because `FR-ENG-034` only emits `EVENT_TAKE_DAMAGE`; meanwhile, mutual combat destruction erroneously emits `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` when an attacker dies to retaliation damage.
  - **Remedy:** Update `FR-ENG-034` Stage 5 to emit both `EVENT_TAKE_DAMAGE` (for the final post-Stage-1 target) and `EVENT_DEAL_DAMAGE` (credited to the attacking/retaliating combatant or `DEAL_DAMAGE.damageSource`) if and only if `(armorAbsorbed + healthDamageCommitted) > 0`; and update `FR-ENG-035` bullet 2 so `EVENT_COMBAT_KILL` is emitted strictly when the defending asset is destroyed by the declared attacker's combat damage in Step 3.

- **[DA-ENG-003] [Gap / Conflict] Missing Immediate CardInstance Transition to STACK Zone Upon Declaration in FR-ENG-006, FR-ENG-012, FR-ENG-013, and FR-ENG-024**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-006`, `FR-ENG-012`, `FR-ENG-013`, `FR-ENG-024`, `FR-ENG-041`, `FR-ENT-002`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `01_Game_Rules_FRD.md` (`FR-ZON-001` line 121, `FR-TRN-003` line 236) mandates that upon declaring a Unit, Structure, or Maneuver, the card **immediately departs its origin zone (`HandZone` or `DiscardZone`) and enters the `Resolution Stack` (`STACK`) zone before additional costs are paid or cost-triggered abilities enqueue**. Although `FR-ENT-002` (line 297) lists `STACK` in `CardInstance.CurrentZone`, `FR-ENG-006` (line 231) omits `StackZone` from `Zones`, and `FR-ENG-012`, `FR-ENG-013`, and `FR-ENG-024` never transition `CardInstance.CurrentZone` from `HAND` or `DISCARD` to `STACK` upon declaration (`FR-ENG-024` line 491 implies a Maneuver card remains in `HandZone` until `FRAME_MANEUVER` finishes executing and moves it to `DiscardZone`).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. When enacting *Steel Rain* (`"Discard up to 3 cards... Then, if you have no cards in hand, draw 2 cards"`) while it is in hand, if *Steel Rain*'s `CardInstance` stays in `HandZone` until `FRAME_MANEUVER` pops and finishes executing, the `handEmpty: true` condition evaluates `count(HandZone) == 1` (*Steel Rain* itself) and fails to draw 2 cards.
    2. When deploying *Veil Render* from `DiscardZone` (`FR-ENG-013`), if *Veil Render*'s `CardInstance` is not moved to `STACK` before executing its `KILL_ASSET` additional cost, any death trigger from the sacrificed ally that inspects `DiscardZone` will still see *Veil Render* in `DiscardZone`, violating `FR-TRN-003`.
  - **Remedy:** Update `FR-ENG-006`, `FR-ENG-012`, `FR-ENG-013`, and `FR-ENG-024` to specify that upon declaring a card deployment or Maneuver enactment, the `CardInstance` immediately transitions from `HandZone` or `DiscardZone` into `ResolutionStack` (`CurrentZone = STACK`) *prior* to executing `KILL_ASSET` / `DISCARD_CARD` / `EXHAUST_ASSET` additional costs in `FR-ENG-013` and prior to resolving `FRAME_DEPLOYMENT` or `FRAME_MANEUVER`.

### 🟡 Medium
- **[DA-ENG-004] [Ambiguity / Underdeveloped Spec] State-Based Death Sweep Trigger Scope (FR-ENG-035) and Intra-Sequence Trigger Buffering (FR-ENG-014, FR-ENG-021)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-014`, `FR-ENG-021`, `FR-ENG-035`, `FR-ENG-041`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:**
    1. `FR-ENG-035` (line 538) states: *"Immediately following damage commitment, the engine MUST execute a state-based death sweep..."*. However, per `FR-LAY-002`, `FR-ENT-103`, and `FR-ENG-041` Invariant 1, lethal destruction also occurs without damage commitment when `MODIFY_STAT` reduces an asset's `EffectiveMaxHealth <= 0` (*Poisoned Rations*), when a continuous health aura ceases (`FR-ENG-023`), or when `MORPH_SCRATCH` transforms a damaged unit into a `1/1` Scratch Company whose existing `AccumulatedDamage >= 1` (*Forced Mutation*).
    2. Neither `FR-ENG-021` nor `FR-ENG-035` specifies whether state-based death sweeps run after **each atomic primitive step** within a `COMPOSITE_SEQUENCE` (*Frostfire Breath*, *Seismic Instability*, *MAX-8 Autonomous Warsuit*), nor whether reactive triggers (`EVENT_DESTROY`, `EVENT_TAKE_DAMAGE`, etc.) emitted during Step 1 of a `COMPOSITE_SEQUENCE` are buffered until the currently resolving `StackFrame` completes all of its steps before being pushed onto `ResolutionStack` via APNAP (`FR-ENG-014`).
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Invoking `FR-ENG-035` only after `FR-ENG-034` (damage commitment) leaves units with `EffectiveMaxHealth <= 0` (*Poisoned Rations*) or morphed damaged units (*Forced Mutation*) alive on the battlefield, or fails to evaluate *Frostfire Breath*'s follow-up `"If that asset is killed, draw a card"` step.
  - **Remedy:** Specify in `FR-ENG-021` and `FR-ENG-035` that: (1) the state-based death sweep (`FR-ENG-035`) executes after every state-mutating atomic primitive step (including `DEAL_DAMAGE`, `MODIFY_STAT`, `MORPH_SCRATCH`, `KILL`, and aura source departure); and (2) while death replacements (`WOULD_BE_DESTROYED`) and `AssetSnapshot` / `DiscardZone` transitions occur immediately after each primitive step, any reactive `TriggeredAbilityDef` instances triggered during a resolving `StackFrame` are buffered and pushed onto `ResolutionStack` in APNAP order at the completion of the currently resolving `StackFrame`.
