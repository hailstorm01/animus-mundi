## 1. Executive Summary & Red-Team Assessment
A rigorous cross-specification red-team audit of [`05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md`](file:///c:/Users/blade/animus-mundi/animus-mundi/docs/frd/05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md) against the authoritative rules specification [`01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md`](file:///c:/Users/blade/animus-mundi/animus-mundi/docs/frd/01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md) (and its sibling data/protocol contracts [`02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md`](file:///c:/Users/blade/animus-mundi/animus-mundi/docs/frd/02_Card_Data_Pipeline_FRD.md) and [`04_Client_Engine_Protocol_Networking_FRD.md`](file:///c:/Users/blade/animus-mundi/animus-mundi/docs/frd/04_Client_Engine_Protocol_Networking_FRD.md)) reveals **3 Blocker**, **4 High**, **1 Medium**, and **1 Low** findings.

While recent revisions aligned the 25 atomic primitives and stack-pushed inherent action routing, significant portions of `05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` still contain **fabricated rules ("hallucinated" genre tropes)** and **direct contradictions** against `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md`. Most critically:
1. **Fabricated Turn Phase Rules (`FR-ENG-009` & Domain 2 Diagram):** The Ready Phase still mandates granting *"base Requisition income"* (`Sub-Phase 1b: Requisition`) and *"resets unspent Tactics"*, while the Domain 2 state machine diagram still mandates `Sub-Phase 4b: Hand Discard` during the End Phase and omits the Main Phase pre-priority Start-of-Turn & Draw Trigger resolution window (`FR-TRN-001`).
2. **Fabricated Replacement Watcher Hierarchy (`FR-ENG-016`):** Invents a rigid `1. Self -> 2. Controller -> 3. Opponent` replacement application order and a once-per-root-event guard that directly invert the 5-stage damage mitigation pipeline (`FR-REP-004`: Stage 1 Redirection before Stage 2/3 Self-Mitigation), strip controller choice on simultaneous conflicts (`FR-REP-001`), and break multi-unit death interception (*Shifting Impossibility*).
3. **Contradictory Stat Clamping Invariants (`FR-ENG-041`, `FR-ENG-028`, `FR-ENG-004`):** Asserts that `"effective defense"` (`MaxHealth` / `CurrentHealth`) and `Resistant` never evaluate to negative integers, directly violating `FR-LAY-001` (*"Combat Power is the only stat clamping at zero. Negative Resistant values do not clamp"*) and breaking `FR-LAY-002` / `FR-REP-004` damage amplification and lethal health checks.
4. **AST & Zone Schema Drift (`FR-ENG-018`–`FR-ENG-020`, `FR-ENG-024`, `FR-ENG-035`):** Invents non-existent zones (`GRAVEYARD` / `GraveyardZone` instead of `DISCARD`), mismatched filter enums (`ENEMY` instead of `OPPOSING`, `HQ_ZONE` instead of `HEADQUARTERS`), and deprecated `ValueToken` types (`CONSTANT`, `COUNT_MATCHING`, `EXHAUSTED_ASSET_COUNT` instead of `STATIC`, `DYNAMIC`, `QUERY`, `VARIABLE_X`).

`05_Headless_Game_Engine_FRD.md` must be revised to eliminate all fabricated rules and schema drift before Phase 3 Go implementation begins.

- **[DA-ENG-001] Fabricated Ready Phase "Base Requisition Income", Ready-Phase Tactics Reset, End Phase "Hand Discard" Diagram, and Omitted Main Phase Pre-Priority Trigger Frame**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-009`, Domain 2 State Machine Diagram (lines 166–192), `FR-TRN-001`, `FR-RES-001`, `FR-RES-002`, `FR-ZON-001`, `FR-ABL-008`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ENG-009` and the Domain 2 state machine diagram contain four direct contradictions with `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md`:
    1. **Made-Up Ready Phase Base Requisition Income (`Sub-Phase 1b: Requisition`):** The Domain 2 diagram (lines 167–168) defines `Sub-Phase 1a: Unexhaust` and `Sub-Phase 1b: Requisition`, and `FR-ENG-009` (line 231) states: *"Phase 1: Ready Phase: Unexhausts all controlled assets in Reserves, Main Theater, and HQ Zone; grants base Requisition income and resets unspent Tactics."* In `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md` (`FR-TRN-001` line 238, `FR-RES-001` line 221, `FR-CAP-001` line 268), Card Town has **zero passive or base Requisition income** during the Ready Phase—resources are generated exclusively during the Main Phase by exhausting Readied assets (`canGenerateResources()`) or via card effects (`GENERATE_RESOURCE`).
    2. **Duplicate/Misplaced Ready-Phase Tactics Reset:** `FR-ENG-009` Phase 1 states the Ready Phase *"resets unspent Tactics"*, contradicting `FR-RES-002` (line 227) and `FR-ENG-009` Sub-Phase 4c (line 237), which already reset both unspent Requisition and Tactics pools to `0` at the end of the turn. Conversely, `FR-ENG-009` Phase 1 omits resetting per-turn ability activation counters (`turnActivationCount = 0` per `FR-ABL-008`).
    3. **Residual `Sub-Phase 4b: Hand Discard` in Domain 2 Diagram:** Although `FR-ENG-009` Sub-Phase 4d prose (line 238) was updated to note uncapped hand capacity (`FR-ZON-001` line 162: *"no maximum hand size"*), the authoritative Domain 2 ASCII state machine diagram (lines 188–192) still instructs engineers to implement `Sub-Phase 4a: End Trigger`, `Sub-Phase 4b: Hand Discard`, and `Sub-Phase 4c: Buff Decay`, contradicting both `FR-ZON-001` (uncapped hand size, zero discard step) and `FR-TRN-001` (`4a: Event Emission & Trigger Enqueuing`, `4b: Trigger & Cascade Resolution`, `4c: Resource Depletion & Expiration`, `4d: Turn Handover`).
    4. **Omitted Main Phase Pre-Priority Start-of-Turn & Draw Trigger Frame (`FR-TRN-001` Step 3):** `FR-TRN-001` (line 241) mandates that upon entering the Main Phase—prior to granting the active player priority for manual action declarations or starting the 75-second Main Phase Turn Clock—all pending *"At the start of your turn"* triggers (*Hammerhand Bivouac*) and Draw Phase triggers (*Portent Scrivener*) enqueue onto the Resolution Stack and auto-resolve to completion via APNAP (`FR-TRG-003`). `FR-ENG-009` (lines 232–233) completely omits this pre-priority trigger resolution window.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. Turn 1 starts. The engine executes `FR-ENG-009` Phase 1 and grants passive "base Requisition income" on top of asset yields, destroying the core readiness economy where players must choose between exhausting their HQ/units for resources vs. combat/Scratch deployment (`Design Rationale DA-RULES-001`).
    2. An engineer implementing the Domain 2 state machine diagram codes a `Hand Discard` step in `Sub-Phase 4b`, forcing players to discard down to an arbitrary cap at end of turn in violation of `FR-ZON-001`.
    3. Active player controls *Hammerhand Bivouac* (`"At the start of your turn..."`) or *Portent Scrivener* (`"Whenever you draw a card..."`). Because `FR-ENG-009` lacks the `FR-TRN-001` Step 3 pre-priority trigger resolution frame at the start of the Main Phase, either the triggers fail to resolve before manual priority opens or block manual actions with `ERR_STACK_NOT_EMPTY`.
  - **Remedy:** Remove `"grants base Requisition income and resets unspent Tactics"` from `FR-ENG-009` Phase 1 and replace with resetting per-turn ability activation counters (`turnActivationCount = 0` per `FR-ABL-008`). Update the Domain 2 ASCII diagram to remove `Sub-Phase 1a/1b` and align `END PHASE` strictly with Sub-Phases `4a`–`4d` (`4a: End Trigger Enqueue`, `4b: Trigger Cascade Resolution`, `4c: Resource & Buff Cleanup`, `4d: Turn Handover`). Add the `FR-TRN-001` Step 3 **Start-of-Turn & Draw Trigger Frame** to `FR-ENG-009` Phase 3 (Main Phase) prior to opening the clean-stack manual action priority window.

- **[DA-ENG-002] Fabricated Replacement Watcher Priority (`Self -> Controller -> Opponent`) & Root-Event Suppression Contradicting `FR-REP-001`–`FR-REP-004`**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-016`, `FR-ENG-034`, `FR-REP-001`, `FR-REP-002`, `FR-REP-003`, `FR-REP-004`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ENG-016` invents a 3-tier **Application Order** (`1. Self-replacement effects -> 2. Controller's replacement effects -> 3. Opponent's replacement effects`) and a **Recursion Guard** (*"A replacement effect MUST NOT apply more than once to the same root event"*) that directly violate `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md`:
    1. **Inversion of 5-Stage Damage Mitigation Precedence (`FR-REP-004`):** Under `FR-REP-004` (lines 460–486) and `FR-REP-001` (line 458), incoming damage interception executes strictly by pipeline stage: **Stage 1: Target Redirection** (e.g. *Animoceps Spore Cloud*, a *controller's* replacement effect on another asset redirecting damage away from an allied unit) MUST execute **before** **Stage 2: Flat `Resistant N`** and **Stage 3: `Stalwart`** (which are *self-replacement* effects on the initial target), so downstream stages evaluate against the new target's defenses. `FR-ENG-016`'s `"1. Self-replacement effects -> 2. Controller's replacement effects"` reverses this order.
    2. **Erasure of Controller Choice on Simultaneous Replacement Conflicts (`FR-REP-001`):** Under `FR-REP-001` (line 455), when multiple replacement watchers attempt to intercept the exact same event within the same stage/hook, **the controller of the affected asset designates the application order**, and once one replacement alters the event so competing conditions are no longer met, competing watchers fizzle. `FR-ENG-016` replaces controller choice with a made-up `Self -> Controller -> Opponent` rule.
    3. **Root-Event vs. Discrete-Event Single Application (`FR-REP-001`, `FR-REP-003`):** `FR-REP-003` (line 454) scopes the single-application rule to *"at most once per discrete event"*, and `FR-REP-001` (line 457) explicitly mandates that for entity destruction interception (*Shifting Impossibility*), *"each asset's prospective destruction emits an individual interceptable death hook, permitting multiple simultaneous entity destructions from a single root damage event to each be replaced sequentially until the intercepting watcher's health is depleted."* `FR-ENG-016`'s ban on applying more than once to the same **root event** breaks *Shifting Impossibility* during multi-unit board wipes.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. Player A controls an allied unit with `Stalwart` (self-replacement) alongside *Animoceps Spore Cloud* (controller replacement: redirect all damage that would be dealt to allied units in the same zone to this structure, which lacks `Stalwart`).
    2. An opponent deals 6 combat damage to the allied unit.
    3. Under `FR-ENG-016`, the unit's self-replacement (`Stalwart`) applies first, reducing the 6 damage to 1, and *Animoceps Spore Cloud* applies second, redirecting only 1 damage to the structure instead of taking the full 6 damage as mandated by `FR-REP-004` Stage 1.
    4. Subsequently, the opponent casts an AoE maneuver that simultaneously deals lethal damage to two allied units while Player A controls *Shifting Impossibility*. Under `FR-ENG-016`'s root-event recursion guard, *Shifting Impossibility* can only save the first unit and is forbidden from intercepting the second unit's prospective destruction because both stem from the same root event.
  - **Remedy:** Replace `FR-ENG-016`'s fabricated `Self -> Controller -> Opponent` ordering and root-event guard with the canonical rules in `FR-REP-001`–`FR-REP-004`: enforce stage-ordered pipeline precedence (`Stage 1 Redirection -> Stage 2 Resistant -> Stage 3 Stalwart -> Stage 4 Armor -> Stage 5 Health Commit & WOULD_BE_DESTROYED`), require controller-of-affected-asset selection (with Single-Option Fast-Path per `FR-MOD-001`) when multiple replacement watchers contend for the same hook/stage, and scope the single-application guard strictly to each discrete interceptable event (`per-entity death hook` for `WOULD_BE_DESTROYED`).

- **[DA-ENG-003] Non-Negative Stat Invariant (`EffectiveStat >= 0`) Contradicts `FR-LAY-001` Exclusive Power Clamping and Breaks Negative `Resistant N` & Lethal Health Checks**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-041` (Invariant 1), `FR-ENG-028`, `FR-ENG-039`, `FR-ENG-004`, `FR-LAY-001`, `FR-LAY-002`, `FR-REP-004`, `FR-CBT-001`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ENG-041` Invariant 1 states: *"Effective power and effective defense MUST never evaluate to negative integers (`EffectiveStat >= 0`)"*, `FR-ENG-028` states that Layer 6 clamping is codified as Invariant 1 in `FR-ENG-041`, `FR-ENG-039` checks for *"negative stat violations"*, and `FR-ENG-004` states *"Damage calculation, flat resistance, armor deductions, and cost reductions MUST NEVER underflow below 0 unless an explicit signed delta contract is invoked."* This directly contradicts `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md`:
    1. **`FR-LAY-001` Exclusive Combat Power Clamping:** `FR-LAY-001` (line 437) explicitly mandates: **`Effective Power = max(0, Raw Power)`. Combat Power is the only stat clamping at zero. Negative Resistant values do not clamp.**
    2. **Negative `Resistant N` Damage Amplification (`FR-REP-004` Stage 2 & `FR-CBT-001`):** Under `FR-REP-004` (line 483) and `FR-CBT-001` (line 500), `Resistant N` can be negative (`Resistant -1`), amplifying even a `0`-power attack to `1` damage (`0 - (-1) = 1`).
    3. **Non-Canonical `"defense"` Term & Negative Health Evaluation (`FR-LAY-002`, `FR-ENG-035`, `FR-ENG-042`):** Card Town has no attribute named `"defense"`; assets have `MaxHealth` and `CurrentHealth = MaxHealth - AccumulatedDamage` (`FR-LAY-002` line 440). Both `FR-LAY-002` and `FR-ENG-035` / `FR-ENG-042` explicitly evaluate `MaxHealth <= 0` (from Layer 5 debuffs like *Poisoned Rations*) and `CurrentHealth <= 0` (from overkill damage) to trigger state-based destruction.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:**
    1. A unit with 2 `MaxHealth` receives a `-3 MaxHealth` debuff in Layer 5 (`EffectiveMaxHealth = -1`), or takes 5 combat damage (`CurrentHealth = -3`).
    2. If `FR-ENG-041` Invariant 1 and `FR-ENG-039` assert `EffectiveStat >= 0` across health/"defense", any overkill hit or lethal health debuff triggers a fatal diagnostic assertion panic before or during `FR-ENG-035` state-based death checks. Conversely, if Layer 6 clamps `Resistant` at `>= 0` under `FR-ENG-004`/`FR-ENG-028`, `Resistant -1` fails to amplify incoming damage in Stage 2 of `FR-REP-004`.
  - **Remedy:** Update `FR-ENG-041` Invariant 1, `FR-ENG-028`, `FR-ENG-039`, and `FR-ENG-004` to match `FR-LAY-001` and `FR-LAY-002`: replace the non-canonical term `"defense"` (`EffectiveDefense`) with `MaxHealth` / `CurrentHealth`; specify that **`CombatPower` (Layer 6 per `FR-LAY-001`), effective deployment costs (`FR-CST-001`), dynamic `Armor` (`FR-REP-004` Stage 4), and post-Stage-2 damage instances clamp at `>= 0`**, whereas `Resistant` is signed (negative values amplify damage in Stage 2) and `MaxHealth` / `CurrentHealth` may evaluate to `<= 0` (immediately triggering state-based destruction in `FR-ENG-035`).

- **[DA-ENG-005] Whole-Frame Fizzle & Card-to-Discard Rule in `FR-ENG-015` Contradicts Composite Clause Resolution, Distributed Pool Evaporation, and Intra-Battlefield Zone Moves (`FR-TGT-003`, `FR-ZON-004`)**
  - **Requirement Citations:** `FR-ENG-015`, Domain 3 Diagram (lines 284–290), `FR-TGT-001`, `FR-TGT-003` (`FR-TGT-103`), `FR-ZON-004`
  - **Vulnerability / Flaw:** `FR-ENG-015` (lines 317–323) contradicts `01_Game_Rules_and_Card_Modeling_FRD.md` in four respects:
    1. **Composite Targeted + Non-Targeted Clauses (`FR-TGT-003` line 352):** `FR-ENG-015` states that when all declared targets become invalid, *"the stack frame fizzles: effect execution is skipped"*. However, `FR-TGT-003` mandates: *"For composite actions containing both targeted clauses and non-targeted global clauses (e.g. All out War), invalidation of all target bindings fizzles only the targeted clauses; non-targeted clauses resolve to the fullest extent possible."*
    2. **Sending Source Card to Discard on All Fizzled Frames (`FR-ENG-015` line 322):** `FR-ENG-015` states that when a stack frame fizzles, *"the card is sent to the discard pile"*. This applies exclusively to `FRAME_MANEUVER` (`FR-MNV-001`). If a `FRAME_ACTIVATED_ABILITY` or `FRAME_TRIGGERED_ABILITY` fizzles due to target invalidation, its source `AssetInstance` remains on the battlefield (`FR-TGT-003` line 354).

    4. **Omitted Distributed Pool Evaporation (`FR-TGT-003` line 353):** `FR-ENG-015`'s Partial Invalidation clause omits the `FR-TGT-003` requirement that for `DISTRIBUTED_POOL` actions (*Macrocannon Barrage*), values allocated to an invalidated target evaporate completely and MUST NOT be redistributed among remaining valid targets.

    2. A battlefield Structure activates an ability (`FRAME_ACTIVATED_ABILITY`) whose target dies before resolution; under a literal reading of `FR-ENG-015` (*"the card is sent to the discard pile"*), the Structure itself is destroyed/discarded.
    3. In *Macrocannon Barrage* (`Distribute 5 damage`), if 1 of 2 targets dies before resolution, lack of the `FR-TGT-003` evaporation constraint risks concentrating all 5 damage onto the surviving target.
  - **Remedy:** Revise `FR-ENG-015` (and the Domain 3 diagram) to conform to `FR-TGT-003` and `FR-ZON-004`: (a) when all targets are invalid, fizzle only targeted clauses while executing any non-targeted clauses of composite effects; (b) transition the card to `DiscardZone` upon completion/fizzle **only** for `FRAME_MANEUVER`; (c) verify that each target still exists as the same `AssetInstance` / `CardInstance` and still satisfies all `FilterDef` constraints (including `FilterDef.zone`); and (d) enforce `DISTRIBUTED_POOL` allocation evaporation without redistribution on partial target invalidation.

- **[DA-ENG-008] Choice Prompt Suspension Scope, Omitted Single-Option Fast-Path, and Reserve Bank Conflation in `FR-ENG-011` & `FR-ENG-017`**

    1. **Omitted Single-Option Fast-Path (`FR-MOD-001` Rule 1, `FR-TRG-002`):** `FR-MOD-001` (line 366) mandates that if exactly one legal target or valid option exists for a required choice, the engine auto-selects it immediately without issuing an interactive `PlayerChoicePrompt` or pausing match execution. `FR-ENG-017` omits this fast-path, unconditionally suspending the stack into `STATE_AWAITING_CHOICE`.
    2. **Upfront Bound Action Payloads vs. Mid-Resolution Prompts (`FR-TGT-101` vs. `FR-ENG-017`):** `FR-ENG-017` (line 333) lists *"modal choice, distributed damage allocation"* as runtime selections that suspend the active stack frame. Under `FR-TGT-101` (lines 356–359), `FR-ABL-010`, and `FR-ABL-011`, modal indices (`selectedModalIndices`) and distributed pool allocations (`targetBindings`) for player-declared actions—as well as upfront-locked external targets for conditional triggers like *Samsara, Living Tempest* (`bindingPolicy: "UPFRONT_LOCK"`)—are bound **upfront** when the action/trigger is placed on the stack, not via mid-resolution suspension. Conversely, `FR-ENG-017` omits the mandatory `REVEAL_HAND` client inspection window (`FR-MOD-001` rule 3, line 368).
    3. **Mulligan Reserve Bank Preservation (`FR-SES-004` vs. `FR-ENG-011` & `FR-ENG-017`):** `FR-ENG-011` item 3 groups the pre-game mulligan timeout fallback (*"keep all cards"*) under Reserve Time Bank expiration, contradicting `FR-SES-004` (line 103: **Pre-Game Reserve Preservation**), which states the 60-second Reserve Time Bank is never drawn down during the 25-second pre-game mulligan. Meanwhile, `FR-ENG-017` item 4 states that in-match choice prompts apply deterministic fallbacks immediately upon expiration of the 15s/10s action clock, omitting the `FR-SES-005` (lines 116–121) rule that the player's remaining 60-second `timeBankMs` drains after the 15s/10s action clock before the deterministic fallback is committed.
  - **Exploit / Failure Scenario:** Every single-option triggered ability needlessly stalls match execution for a 3,000 ms grace window plus network RTT; players who take more than 15s/10s on a complex `LOOK_TOP_CARDS` partition have their choice auto-fallback immediately even when they have 60 seconds of `timeBankMs` remaining (`FR-SES-005`); and players who time out during the pre-game mulligan enter Turn 1 with 0 seconds of Reserve Time Bank (`FR-SES-004`).
  - **Remedy:** Update `FR-ENG-017` and `FR-ENG-011` to: (a) enforce the `FR-MOD-001` Single-Option Fast-Path and `REVEAL_HAND` inspection window; (b) distinguish upfront bound action/trigger payloads (`FR-TGT-101`: manual modal choices, `DISTRIBUTED_POOL` allocations, `UPFRONT_LOCK` conditional targets) from mid-resolution prompts (`LOOK_TOP_CARDS`, `SEARCH_DECK`, `REVEAL_HAND`, and non-upfront reactive trigger choices); and (c) align timeout progression with `FR-SES-004` (mulligan bypasses `timeBankMs`) and `FR-SES-005` (in-match prompts drain 15s/10s action clock &rarr; `timeBankMs` &rarr; `FR-MOD-001` deterministic fallback).
